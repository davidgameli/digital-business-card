# 💼 Digital Business Card

A sleek and professional digital business card designed to share your contact info and online presence with ease and style. 🌐📇  
Ideal for freelancers, job seekers, and professionals who want a modern, shareable way to make a strong first impression.

🔗 **Live Demo:** [digital-business-card](https://davidgameli.github.io/digital-business-card/)

## 🎯 Features

- 📬 Displays name, title, and contact details
- 🔗 Clickable links to social media and portfolio
- 📱 Fully responsive design for all screen sizes
- 🧾 Clean, minimalist, and easy to customize
